Privacy Policy

Practical Field Meter, by Practical Productions
Effective date: October 24, 2026

Summary

Practical Field Meter does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send information off your device. It has no ads, no analytics, no user accounts, and no tracking.

Microphone

Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. The microphone is used only by the sound level meter, and only after you allow it. The app turns each tenth of a second of sound into a single number (the sound level in decibels) and discards the sound itself: its working buffer is overwritten by the next tenth of a second. No audio is ever written to storage or transmitted. Android shows its microphone-in-use indicator while the sound meter is running. You can switch the sound meter off in the app's settings, or refuse or remove the microphone permission in Android settings; the other meters keep working.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Sessions you choose to record: the start and end time, the sensor readings taken during the session (magnetic field, sound level in decibels, light level, vibration), and any markers and short notes you add.
  • Your settings: sample rate, alert settings, whether the sound meter is on, the sound calibration offset, and whether the app has already asked for the microphone.

This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself, and Practical Productions has no access to it. The app excludes it from cloud backups and from transfers to a new phone. You can delete any session in the app, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Exporting sessions

You can export a session as a CSV file. The file goes only where you choose to save or send it, using Android's own file picker or share sheet. Once you send a file to another app or person, that app's or person's handling of it is outside this app.

Permissions

The app asks for two permissions. Microphone (asked the first time you open the sound meter) is optional; without it, the sound meter stays off and everything else works. Vibration lets the optional magnetic spike alert vibrate the phone; it is granted automatically and accesses no data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
